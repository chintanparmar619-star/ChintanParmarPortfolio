We didn't launch a brand.
We launched a character.

The market was full. That was the problem.

Another brand doing the same thing wasn't going to work.

People don't remember labels. They remember personalities. So before anyone saw a single NOMHERD product, we built an identity — a tone, a world, a way of showing up that felt like someone, not something.

The Thinking

That became the foundation for everything. Content strategy, keyword decisions, influencer selection, how the web campaign was structured — all of it came from one place: who is NOMHERD, not what does it sell.

The launch video wasn't what anyone expected. No lookbook. No model walking down a street. NOMHERD went animated.

Animation gave us something a shoot couldn't — control over the entire world the brand lived in. The character could breathe. The personality could show before the product did.

Influencers + Rollout

Influencers came in after the identity was set. Most brands use influencers to introduce themselves. We didn't. By the time influencers spoke about NOMHERD, the brand already had a voice — they amplified it, they didn't define it.

Then came the photos. The photo shoot content rolled out after. Same brand, now in the real world. Because the audience had already met the character, every post felt like a continuation of something — not a cold introduction.

What I Did

  • Built NOMHERED's content strategy from positioning to post cadence
  • Handled SEO and keyword research focused on cargo-specific search intent in India
  • Planned and structured the launch campaign — animated video, reels, rollout sequence
  • Developed the web campaign — how the brand story connected across pages and discovery
  • Managed influencer marketing — selection, brief, and integration into the brand narrative
  • Strategised the photo shoot rollout as a second act, not a standalone drop

Outcome

Created a consistent brand character and carried that identity across strategy, content, launch video, influencer communication, web and the photo-shoot rollout.
